# Government Schemes for Farmers in India

Information verified against official and government sources on 2026-11-24. Scheme rules, amounts and deadlines change. Always confirm on the official portal, at your bank, Raitha Samparka Kendra, Common Service Centre (CSC) or Krishi Vigyan Kendra before applying.

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
Category: Income support. Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.

**Benefit:** ₹6,000 per year paid in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the Aadhaar-seeded bank account of eligible landholding farmer families.

**Eligibility:**
- Landholding farmer family (husband, wife and minor children) with cultivable land recorded in State/UT land records.
- Land ownership records are verified; land acquired after 01-02-2019 is under special scrutiny.
- Excluded: institutional landholders; families where a member is or was a constitutional post holder, minister, MP/MLA, mayor or district panchayat chairperson; serving or retired government employees (except Group D / multi-tasking staff); pensioners with monthly pension ≥ ₹10,000 (except Group D); income-tax payers in the last assessment year; registered doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects practising professionally.

**Documents needed:** Aadhaar, Land records (RTC/Pahani in Karnataka), Aadhaar-seeded bank account, Mobile number.

**How to apply:**
- Register on pmkisan.gov.in (New Farmer Registration) or through a CSC / village agriculture officer.
- Complete e-KYC (OTP, face authentication in the PM-KISAN app, or biometric at a CSC) — mandatory for receiving instalments.
- Check beneficiary status on the portal using Aadhaar or registration number.

Official website: https://pmkisan.gov.in

## PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) — crop insurance
Category: Insurance. Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.

**Benefit:** Insurance cover for yield loss from natural calamities, pests and diseases, prevented sowing, localised risks and post-harvest losses. Farmer premium is capped at 2% of sum insured for Kharif food and oilseed crops, 1.5% for Rabi food and oilseed crops, and 5% for annual commercial/horticultural crops; the rest is paid by Central and State governments (50:50; 90:10 in North-Eastern and Himalayan states).

**Eligibility:**
- All farmers, including sharecroppers and tenant farmers, growing notified crops in notified areas.
- Voluntary for all farmers since Kharif 2020 (loanee farmers can opt out through their bank).

**Documents needed:** Aadhaar, Bank account details, Land records or tenancy/sowing certificate, Sowing declaration.

**How to apply:**
- Enrol through your bank, CSC, insurance company agent or the National Crop Insurance Portal (pmfby.gov.in) before the seasonal cut-off date notified for your district (typically July for Kharif and December for Rabi).
- Report localised loss (e.g. hailstorm, inundation) to the insurer, bank or agriculture department within 72 hours, e.g. through the Crop Insurance app.

Official website: https://pmfby.gov.in

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC) with Modified Interest Subvention Scheme
Category: Credit. Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / RBI / NABARD.

**Benefit:** Revolving short-term credit for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses and allied activities (animal husbandry, dairy, fisheries). Under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS) crop loans are given at 7% per year, and farmers who repay promptly get a further 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive — an effective rate of 4%. The Union Budget 2025-26 announced raising the MISS loan limit from ₹3 lakh to ₹5 lakh; check with your bank how this applies to your account.

**Eligibility:**
- Owner-cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, and self-help groups or joint liability groups of farmers.
- Farmers engaged in animal husbandry and fisheries are also eligible.

**Documents needed:** Application form, Identity and address proof (Aadhaar), Land records / tenancy proof, Passport photograph, Cropping pattern details.

**How to apply:**
- Apply at any commercial bank, regional rural bank or cooperative bank branch, or through the bank's online portal.
- The credit limit is fixed from the scale of finance for your crops and your land area.

Official website: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/kcc

## PMKSY – Per Drop More Crop (micro-irrigation)
Category: Irrigation. Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.

**Benefit:** Financial assistance for drip and sprinkler irrigation: 55% of the unit cost for small and marginal farmers and 45% for other farmers, for up to 5 hectares per beneficiary. Many states, including Karnataka, add a top-up subsidy.

**Eligibility:**
- Farmers with their own land (or lease agreement where allowed) and a water source.
- Small and marginal farmers receive the higher assistance rate.

**Documents needed:** Aadhaar, Land records (RTC), Bank details, Water source proof, Quotation/design from a registered micro-irrigation company.

**How to apply:**
- Apply through the State horticulture or agriculture department (in Karnataka, through the Raitha Samparka Kendra / horticulture office or the state online portal).
- A registered supplier prepares the design and installs; subsidy is released after field verification.

Official website: https://pmksy.gov.in

## Soil Health Card Scheme
Category: Soil. Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.

**Benefit:** Free soil testing of farm samples with a card reporting 12 parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B, pH, EC, organic carbon) and crop-wise fertiliser and amendment recommendations.

**Eligibility:**
- All farmers.

**Documents needed:** Farmer details and survey number of the plot.

**How to apply:**
- Soil samples are collected by the agriculture department in campaign mode; you can also contact the nearest soil testing laboratory or Raitha Samparka Kendra.
- Download your card from the Soil Health Card portal.

Official website: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in

## e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)
Category: Marketing. Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (SFAC).

**Benefit:** Online trading platform linking APMC mandis so farmers can sell to buyers beyond their local market with transparent price discovery and online payment.

**Eligibility:**
- Farmers, FPOs and traders registered on the e-NAM portal or at an e-NAM-integrated APMC.

**Documents needed:** Aadhaar, Bank account details, Mobile number.

**How to apply:**
- Register at enam.gov.in or the e-NAM mobile app, or at the e-NAM help desk of an integrated mandi.

Official website: https://enam.gov.in

## Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
Category: Credit / Infrastructure. Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.

**Benefit:** Medium to long-term loans for post-harvest management and community farming assets (warehouses, cold storage, sorting/grading units, primary processing) with 3% per year interest subvention for up to 7 years on loans up to ₹2 crore, plus credit guarantee cover (CGTMSE / NABSanrakshan) for loans up to ₹2 crore. It is not a grant.

**Eligibility:**
- Farmers, FPOs, PACS, self-help groups, joint liability groups, cooperatives, agri-entrepreneurs and start-ups.

**Documents needed:** Detailed project report, KYC, Land documents, Bank account.

**How to apply:**
- Apply on agriinfra.dac.gov.in and choose a participating lending institution.

Official website: https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in

## PM-KMY (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maan-Dhan Yojana) — farmer pension
Category: Social security. Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.

**Benefit:** Minimum assured pension of ₹3,000 per month after age 60. The farmer contributes ₹55–₹200 per month depending on entry age and the Central Government contributes an equal amount. The spouse is entitled to 50% as family pension.

**Eligibility:**
- Small and marginal farmers owning up to 2 hectares of cultivable land as per State/UT land records.
- Entry age 18 to 40 years.
- Excluded: those covered by other statutory social security schemes (NPS, ESIC, EPFO) and higher-income categories as per scheme guidelines.

**Documents needed:** Aadhaar, Savings bank account / PM-KISAN account, Land records.

**How to apply:**
- Enrol at a Common Service Centre (CSC) or on maandhan.in.

Official website: https://maandhan.in
